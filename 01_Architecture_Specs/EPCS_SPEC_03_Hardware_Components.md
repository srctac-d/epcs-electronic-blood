## 1. Electrochemical Core (VRFB Stack)
* **Vanadium Electrolyte:** Aqueous V2+/V3+ and V4+/V5+ solutions in sulfuric acid carrier fluid[cite: 4].
* **Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM):** High-selectivity fluoropolymer ion-exchange membrane (e.g., Nafion).
* **Graphite Felt Electrodes & Bipolar Plates:** Highly conductive, acid-resistant flow-field plates.

## 2. Thermal & Hydraulic Loop ("Circulatory System")
* **Electrolyte Storage Tanks:** HDPE/PVDF chemically inert dual-reservoir tanks.
* **Variable-Speed Chemical Pumps:** Magnetic-drive, seal-less acid pumps.
* **Direct-to-Chip Cold Plates:** Microchannel fluidic manifolds mounted directly to server silicon.
* **Telemetry Array:** Integrated flow meters, pH sensors, thermocouples, and inline pressure transducers.
