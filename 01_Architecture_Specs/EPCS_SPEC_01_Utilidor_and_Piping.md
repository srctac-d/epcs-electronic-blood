## 1. System Overview

Traditional high-performance compute (HPC) facilities connect grid transformers directly to server halls via heavy copper busbars and metallic conduit. This creates two structural vulnerabilities:
1. **Electromagnetic Vulnerability:** Metallic pathways act as passive antennas, propagating grid surges, solar CME pulses, or high-altitude EMP (E1/E3) events directly to server silicon[cite: 4, 5, 6].
2. **Thermal & Fire Risk:** High-amperage copper distribution generates resistive heat directly inside the compute environment and requires massive transformer footprints[cite: 4, 5, 6].

The **EPCS Subterranean Utilidor System** replaces copper power distribution lines with a **4-pipe non-conductive fluidic trunk** housed inside pre-cast or modular **6,000 PSI Geocrete subterranean trenches**[cite: 2, 4, 5].
