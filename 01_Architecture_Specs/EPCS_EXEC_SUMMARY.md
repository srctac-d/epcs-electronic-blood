## 1. Problem Statement
* **AI & HPC Energy Demands:** Massive energy consumption driven by high-density AI workloads and high-performance computing.
* **Supply Chain & Thermal Bottlenecks:** Global shortage of copper transformers paired with intense localized heat generated within facility racks.
* **Single-Purpose Inefficiencies:** Legacy infrastructure relies on isolated, single-purpose power and cooling networks that incur compounding energy losses.

---

## 2. Proposed Solution
* An integrated **Electrochemical Power and Cooling System (EPCS)** that leverages dual-use Vanadium Redox Flow ($V^{2+}/V^{3+}$ and $VO^{2+}/VO_{2}^{+}$) chemistry—acting as "electronic blood" to deliver point-of-load power buffering and direct microchannel liquid cooling simultaneously.

---

## 3. Key Accomplishments & Benefits
* **Dual-Use Efficiency:** Maximizes system core utility by unifying thermal management and power delivery into a single liquid flow loop.
* **PUE Improvement:** Substantially lowers overall facility energy consumption by bypassing traditional, energy-intensive mechanical chillers.
* **Direct-to-Chip Heat Dissipation:** Uses circulating liquid electrolyte to capture and dissipate heat directly from high-density server silicon.
* **Grid Resilience & LDES:** Provides grid-decoupled long-duration energy storage (LDES) without diesel emissions or heavy reliance on transformers.
* **Subterranean Distribution:** Integrates with non-conductive Geocrete utilidor trenches to isolate high-density hydraulic trunks and enhance EMP/physical hardness.
* **Enhanced Safety:** Utilizes a non-flammable, aqueous electrolyte ideal for indoor and critical infrastructure deployment.
